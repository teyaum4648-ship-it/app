A few things to know

Good questions.

What is Daywoven?

Daywoven is a wellness app in development, designed to bring sleep, food, movement, and your everyday mental and lifestyle context into one clearer picture.

What happens when I sign up?

You'll join the early-access interest list and may receive product news or invitations as Daywoven develops. Joining the list doesn't create an app account.

Do I need a wearable?

No wearable is required for the concept shown here. Daywoven is being designed around simple, everyday entries. Available integrations may change as the product develops.

How does the daily guidance work?

The example guidance connects your logged habits and context into an estimated daily outlook. It's designed to support reflection, not to claim direct measurement of your body.

Is Daywoven medical advice?

No. Daywoven is intended for general wellness and educational purposes, not diagnosis, treatment, or medical decision-making. The screens on this page are illustrative.
