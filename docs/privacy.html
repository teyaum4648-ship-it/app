How we use your email.

When you submit your email with the consent checkbox selected, Daywoven saves your email address and the date and time of signup in a Cloudflare D1 database hosted in the United States. We use these details to manage early-access updates and invitations. Signing up does not create an app account.

Cloudflare provides our website and database infrastructure. We do not sell your email address or collect personal health information through this form. We retain your signup until you unsubscribe or the early-access list is retired. Any future emails will include unsubscribe instructions. We will provide a way to request deletion before starting an email campaign.

Do not submit personal health information through this website.
